SWR Pipes

uPVC pipes for non-pressure drainage of soil, waste and rainwater as per IS 13592 – light, medium and heavy duty types.[web:29][web:58]

Pipe Types

SWR – Soil, Waste & Rainwater
  • Type A: Light duty – vents & rainwater applications.[web:29]
  • Type B: Medium duty – internal soil & waste lines.[web:29]
  • Type C: Heavy duty – external & exposed drainage.[web:29]
  • SWR Single Socket Pipes with rubber ring (ring-fit).[web:58]
